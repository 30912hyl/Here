Common questions

Why do I need a phone number?

Reading is open to everyone. Posting and chatting need a verified number so that every account belongs to a real person and a ban actually sticks. Your number is never shown to anyone.

Where did my post go?

Posts leave the feed after 48 hours. You can still see yours under Profile → My Posts → Archived.

Why did my conversation end?

Conversations last 48 hours. Near the end, both people are asked whether to continue for another day; it continues only if both say yes.

Someone is making me uncomfortable.

In a conversation, tap Help → Block & End, or Report & End. On a post, tap ⋯ → Report Post or Block User. Blocking is instant and the other person is not told.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. This removes your sign-in, phone number, posts and photos.

I was banned and I think it was a mistake.

Email us from the address above with the phone number you signed up with and we will review it.

If you are in crisis

Here is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. In the United States, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
