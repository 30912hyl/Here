Here is an anonymous space to share how you feel and talk privately with someone who understands. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. “We” means {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, the operator of Here.

The short version

  • You can read posts without giving us anything about yourself.
  • To post or chat we ask for a phone number. It is used to verify you and to keep banned people out. It is never shown to other users.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account, and the content tied to it, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

WhatWhy
An anonymous account identifier, created on first launchSo the app can show your own posts, conversations and settings
Your phone number (only if you choose to post, chat or use voice)To verify you are a real person, let you sign back in on a new device, and enforce bans

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifier.

What other users can see

Other users see your posts and, in a conversation, the messages you send and a randomly generated nickname. They never see your phone number or account identifier. Nicknames are different in every conversation, so two people you talk to cannot tell they were talking to the same person.

Anything you write is visible to whoever can read it, and they could copy or screenshot it. Please do not share information that identifies you or someone else.

How long we keep things

  • Posts leave the feed 48 hours after they are posted. You can still see your own expired posts under My Posts. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Conversations end after 48 hours unless both people choose to continue. Messages you sent stay visible to the other person in that conversation.
  • Reports and the content attached to them are kept for as long as needed to enforce our rules and meet legal obligations.
  • Banned phone numbers are kept so that a banned person cannot simply sign up again.

Who we share data with

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) to run the app: sign-in, database, photo storage, and push notifications. Google processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms. Phone-number verification is sent by SMS through Google’s providers.

We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your sign-in, your phone number, your posts and photos, and your settings. Messages you already sent remain in the other person’s conversation until it expires, no longer linked to any account.
  • Sign out: Profile → Sign Out returns you to read-only, anonymous browsing.
  • Block or report: from the ⋯ menu on a post, or Help in a conversation.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Here.
  • Access or deletion requests: email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Contact us and we will respond.

Age

Here is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor is using Here, tell us and we will remove the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure provider. Access rules on our database prevent users from reading other people’s conversations, phone numbers, reports, or block lists. No system is perfectly secure; please use care with what you share.

If you are in crisis

Here is not an emergency or counselling service and posts are not monitored in real time. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
